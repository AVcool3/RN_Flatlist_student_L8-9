# GEV Baseline — what we know as of 2026-09-25

This is the starting picture the hourly bot builds on. Everything below is sourced;
numbers in the tables are what feed the stock pitch. Update this file when a
quarterly print changes the picture (next one: **Q3 2026 on Oct 28, 2026**).

## 1. The "Jan 2027 announcement" you are positioning for

GEV reports Q4/FY2026 in late January 2027 (Q4 2025 was Jan 28, 2026). That print is where
management will (a) confirm gas GW under contract vs the **>=125 GW year-end 2026 target**,
(b) give 2027 revenue / shipment guidance, and (c) update how much of 2030 and 2031
production is sold. Conviction on the pitch comes from tracking whether the run-rate of
new contracts between now and then supports those targets.

## 4. Where shipments / orders are going (geography)

Named in 2026 disclosures and press releases:
- **USA** — Homer City (7x 7HA.02, deliveries 2026), the bulk of data-center demand, onshore wind repowering.
- **Saudi Arabia** — Taiba 1 & Qassim 1 (6x 7HA.03 + 2x 7E.03, 21-yr services); Jafurah cogen (7HA.01) in service Nov 2025.
- **Vietnam, Mexico, Brazil, Canada** — named in Q1 2026 gas signings.
- **Qatar, Taiwan** — "active discussions" per management.
- **Venezuela** — Sept 2026 framework: 1 GW in 24 mo + 5 GW over 4 yrs (generation + T&D). Value undisclosed; payment-guarantee risk.
- **Egypt** — services (9F rotor life extension) Sept 2026.
- **Japan, Italy, Germany, UK, Taiwan** — onshore wind orders (small MW, but shows international wind demand).

Gap to close: GEV does not disclose GW shipped by region. The bot's job is to build that
picture bottom-up from press releases and filings.

## 5. Company-level backdrop

- Q2 2026: orders $24.2B (+88% organic), revenue $11.1B, backlog $176B (on track for $200B in 2027), FCF $5.1B in the quarter.
- Q2 RPO: $176.3B total = $87.8B equipment (36% / 65% / 97% recognized within 1 / 2 / 5 yrs) + $88.5B services.
- Contract liabilities $39.9B (vs $25.8B at YE 2025) — this is largely customer prepayments on slot reservations. **Watch this line each 10-Q: it is the hardest evidence that SRAs are real cash.**
- 2026 guidance raised: revenue $45.5-46.5B, FCF $11.5-12.5B, adj. EBITDA margin 12-14%.

## 6. Risks / things the bot will flag as "issues"

- Slot reservations are not firm orders; conversion rate (6 GW Q1, 10 GW Q2) is the metric to watch.
- Venezuela: sanctions / payment terms; do not count it in backlog until an 8-K or firm PO.
- Offshore wind: Vineyard Wind settled, but the segment remains a loss center.
- Supply chain: 2029-30 sold out means upside is capacity, not demand — watch capex announcements.
- Competitors (Siemens Energy, Mitsubishi Power) winning marquee data-center deals = share-loss signal.
