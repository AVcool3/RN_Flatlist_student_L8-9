- What is included in Premium?

Select Premium plans now include 15 hours of audiobook listening time every month from our subscriber catalog of over 700,000 titles. Unused listening hours expire at the end of each month and do not carry over. [Learn more about our Premium plans](https://www.spotify.com/premium/?ref=audiobooks).

- Do I need a Premium plan to access audiobooks on Spotify?

No, you can also get access through purchasing individual audiobooks from the Spotify Web Player ( [Learn more](https://support.spotify.com/us/article/audiobooks-purchase/)) or subscribing to the Audiobooks Access plan ( [Learn more](https://support.spotify.com/us/article/audiobooks-access-plan/)).

- Can I buy audiobooks in the Spotify app?

For now, audiobooks on Spotify can only be purchased in the Spotify Web Player, but you can listen in the app once you’ve unlocked a title. Head to [open.spotify.com](https://open.spotify.com/genre/0JQ5DAqbMKFETqK4t8f1n3?b2mwp=true), find an audiobook you want to listen to, and follow the steps to check out. Then head back to the app on any device to start listening.

- How does the Audiobooks Access plan work?

With the Audiobooks Access plan, you now get 15 hours of audiobook listening time every month from our audiobooks subscriber catalog. Unused listening hours expire at the end of each month and do not carry over. Your music listening experience will be on our free, ad-supported service. If you’re an existing Premium subscriber or a Premium plan member, you’ll need to cancel your current plan first before joining Audiobooks Access. [Learn more](https://support.spotify.com/article/audiobooks-access-plan/).

Note: Audiobooks Access plan is not currently supported on certain devices including Apple Watch, Garmin, Samsung Watches, Tesla, and Car Thing.

- I’m an author, how do I get my audiobook on Spotify?

If your audiobook rights belong to a publisher, ask them if they’re distributing on Spotify. If you are an independent or self-published author, the easiest way to make your audiobook available on Spotify is through [Spotify for Authors](https://authors.spotify.com/).

- I’m on a Duo or Family plan but do not see the option to stream Audiobooks as part of my plan. How can I access audiobook listening time?

If you're a member of a Premium Duo or Premium Family plan, you can access the audiobook subscriber catalog by [requesting an add-on](https://www.spotify.com/account/subscription/change?tab=1). Your plan manager, who manages your subscription, can choose to give you access to this add-on. [Explore add-ons here](https://www.spotify.com/account/subscription/change?tab=1).

- If I have access to the audiobook subscriber catalog, what happens when I have fully consumed my allotted hours for the month?

If you fully consume your plan’s monthly allotted time, you can purchase a top-up, available in increments of 10 hours, that allows you to keep listening to audiobooks before your next month's renewal. Get more details [here](https://support.spotify.com/us/article/audiobook-listening-time-purchase/).
