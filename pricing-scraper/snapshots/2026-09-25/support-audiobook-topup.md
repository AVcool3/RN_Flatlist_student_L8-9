# Purchasing audiobook listening time

Some Premium plans include monthly audiobook listening time from the audiobooks subscriber catalog (see table below). To get more audiobook listening time, you can:

- Subscribe to a monthly Audiobooks+ add-on
- Purchase a one-time top-up

**Note:** Family and Duo plan members must request audiobook listening time from their plan manager via the “Audiobooks+ for Plan Members” add-on or via a top-up.

|  | **Payment** | **Plan eligibility** | **Hours expiry** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Plan hours** | Monthly (included in Premium) | - Premium Individual<br>- Premium Family (plan manager only)<br>- Premium Duo (plan manager only) | Resets monthly.<br>Used first. |
| **Audiobooks+** | Monthly | - Premium Individual<br>- Premium Family (plan manager only)<br>- Premium Duo (plan manager only) | Resets monthly.<br>Used after plan hours run out. |
| **Audiobooks+ for Plan members** | Monthly | - Premium Family (plan members only)<br>- Premium Duo (plan members only) | Resets monthly. |
| **Top-up** | One-time | - Premium Individual<br>- Premium Family (everyone in the plan)<br>- Premium Duo (everyone in the plan) | Valid for 12 months from purchase.<br>Used after plan hours and Audiobooks+ hours run out. |

## Audiobooks+ add-ons

Audiobooks+ is a recurring add-on subscription added to your Premium plan. Its price is added to your monthly Premium subscription in one bill.

- **Audiobooks+** adds extra audiobook listening hours to your own Premium plan each month
- **Audiobooks+ for Plan Members** lets plan managers add audiobook listening time for Duo or Family plan members each month

- ### How to subscribe to Audiobooks+

You can subscribe to add-ons with a credit/debit card, PayPal, or Google Play Billing.

1. Go to **[Available subscriptions](https://www.spotify.com/account/subscription/change/).**
2. Check the **Add-ons** tab to see what’s available to you.
3. Select **Add** next to the chosen add-on, and follow the steps to complete the purchase.

**Note**: Adding Audiobooks+ moves your billing date forward, and your Premium subscription cost will be adjusted proportionally. If you cancel your Premium subscription, you’ll lose access to any purchased add-ons.

## Audiobook top-ups

Top-ups are one-time purchases and provide extra audiobook listening hours. They’re valid for 12 months from the purchase date, and you can add more than one at a time.

Top-ups are used only when your monthly plan and Audiobooks+ add-on hours run out.

- ### How to purchase top-ups

You can purchase top-ups with a credit/debit card, PayPal, or through Google Play Billing.

1. Go to [**Audiobook listening time**](https://www.spotify.com/account/audiobook-listening-time/).
2. Under **Top-ups**, select **Top up**.

For more information on our refund policy, you can check our [Refund policy for purchased audiobook listening hours](https://support.spotify.com/us/article/refund-policy-audiobook-hours/). Other [Terms and Conditions](https://www.spotify.com/legal/paid-subscription-terms/) apply.

**Notes**:

  - You must have an eligible Premium plan to use your top-up hours
  - If you cancel your subscription, you’ll no longer have access to your top-up hours unless you resubscribe within 12 months of purchasing the top-up
  - Your top-up hours will still be subject to their original expiration date

## Managing audiobook listening time for Family and Duo plans

Only plan managers can purchase add-ons and top-ups for Family and Duo plan members.

### If you’re the plan manager

- ### How to purchase a recurring add-on for plan members

You can purchase a recurring add-on for your Family or Duo plan members with a credit/debit card, PayPal, or through Google Play Billing.

**Note**: If you pay with Google Play Billing, you can only purchase Audiobooks+ for one of your plan members.

1. Go to **[Available Subscriptions](https://www.spotify.com/account/subscription/change/)**.
2. Go to the **Add-ons** tab, and select **Add** next to the chosen add-on.
3. Select the member(s) you want to give an add-on to, and tap **Continue**.
4. Review your new subscription details, and tap **Add to subscription**.

- ### How to purchase a top-up for plan members

You can purchase one-time top-ups for your Family or Duo plan members with a credit/debit card, PayPal, or through Google Play Billing.

1. Go to [**Manage members**](https://www.spotify.com/account/family/).
2. Scroll to **Plan members**, and select the member(s) you want to give top-ups to.
3. Under **Manage audiobook listening**, select **Purchase**.

**Note**: If a member leaves their Family or Duo plan, they’ll lose access to any purchased top-ups. They can use top-ups again if they rejoin the plan or start an eligible subscription. Top-ups will still be subject to their original expiration date.

### If you’re a plan member

- ### How to request an add-on to the plan manager

Only the manager of Premium Family or Duo can subscribe members to a recurring **Audiobooks+ for Plan Members** add-on, but plan members can request it through the app or their account page.

For each request, the plan manager receives an email and an in-app notification.

**Note:** If a member leaves their Family or Duo plan, the add-on will be removed from the subscription and the member will lose access to any add-ons.

#### Mobile

1. Tap your profile picture.
2. Tap **Your Premium**.
3. Select **Manage**.
4. Select **Explore plans**.
5. Select the **Add-ons** tab.
6. Tap **Request** next to the chosen add-on.
7. Tap **Request access** to notify your plan manager.

#### Web

1. Go to [**Available subscriptions**](https://www.spotify.com/account/subscription/change/).
2. Under the **Add-ons** tab, select **Request** next to the chosen add-on.
3. Select **Request access** to notify your plan manager.

- ### How to request a top-up to the plan manager

Only the manager of Premium Family or Duo can purchase top-ups for plan members, but plan members can request it through the app or their account page.

For each request, your plan manager receives an email and an in-app notification.

#### Mobile

1. Tap your profile picture.
2. Tap **Your Premium**.
3. Select **Audiobook listening time**.
4. Tap **Request hours**.

#### Web

1. Go to [**Audiobook listening time**](https://www.spotify.com/account/audiobook-listening-time/).
2. Select **Request hours**.

## Viewing audiobook listening details

You can view the details of available audiobook listening time on your account:

- Listening hours remaining (including any add-on hours if applicable)
- Expiry date for all unused top-ups

**Note**: Once you’ve used up a top-up, the details are automatically removed from this view.

- ### Mobile

1. Tap your profile picture at the top.
2. Tap **Your Premium**.
3. Select **Audiobook listening time**.

- ### Web

Go to [**Audiobook listening time**](https://www.spotify.com/account/audiobook-listening-time/).

## Canceling audiobook listening hours

You can cancel at any time.

- **If you cancel your Premium subscription:** You'll lose access to any purchased add-ons and any unused top-up hours.
- **If you cancel an Audiobooks+ add-on:** You'll lose the extra monthly add-on hours but keep your plan's included hours.
- **If you cancel a top-up:** If you resubscribe within 12 months of purchasing a top-up, your remaining top-up hours become available again (still subject to their original expiration date).
- **If a plan member leaves Family or Duo:** The member loses access to any add-ons and top-ups purchased for them. Top-ups can be used again if they rejoin the plan or start an eligible subscription (still subject to the original expiration date).
